## Goal

Process every enabled `detailer` or `both` region sequentially while retaining
the changes produced by earlier jobs.

## Minimal wiring

1. Connect **BV Regional Prompt** to **BV Regional Detailer Plan**.
2. Optionally connect **BV Detector Registry** through the V3 resource path.
3. Connect the plan and initial image to **BV Detailer Loop Start**.
4. Resolve each job with **BV Detailer Loop Job Resolver**.
5. Use the composed region mask directly or refine it through
   **BV Detailer Loop Detect to SEGS (Impact)**.
6. Process the image with the selected detailer.
7. Send the processed image to **BV Detailer Loop End**.

## Detector boundary

The registry owns configured detectors. Detection runs against a padded regional
crop, returned coordinates are rebased to the full image, and the result is gated
by the exact region mask.

## Internal nodes

The four hidden While, Advance, and Result nodes are created during graph
expansion. Users must not place, remove, or rewire them manually.
